# finalprojectthesis

Code for my bachelor's thesis work and a related side project.

| Folder | What it is | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`ateria/`](ateria/) | **Ateria**: a weekly meal planner with a food log and a shopping list of amounts to buy (Finland). Works in English, Finnish, Swedish and Vietnamese. | Working (version 2) |

## Try Ateria in 30 seconds

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `ateria/index.html` in a browser (double-click is enough). No install, no server, no internet needed except for the fonts.
3. Press **You**, enter your details, press **Create my plan**.

More detail, including the tests, is in [`ateria/README.md`](ateria/README.md).

## Key facts

- Builds a 7-day meal plan from your calorie and protein targets (lose, maintain or gain weight) and a shopping list that says how much of each ingredient the meals need, for the days you choose.
- 66 recipes adapted from recipes published by dietitians, universities and health agencies (Nordic, European, Asian and everyday dishes) with amounts in spoons, dl and pieces for 1 to 8 portions, and seasoning listed by the moment it goes in.
- Four languages and pictures for every ingredient, so the main steps can be followed without reading much.
- Your details are saved on your device only if you agree. You can back them up, restore them or delete them. A Help button answers "how do I...?" questions.
- **Nutrition values are placeholders** until they are replaced with a licensed database such as Fineli. The app shows amounts, not prices.
- Not medical advice. For adults only (18-99).
